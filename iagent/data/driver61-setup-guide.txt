# 🏁 SIM RACING SETUP GUIDE (Driver61)

Use this guide to fine-tune your car and maximize your on-track performance with clear and simple tweaks.

---

## 🌀 CORNERS

### ⚡ Corner Entry (Braking + Initial Turn-In)

#### **Understeer (Car won’t turn enough)**

* Increase front negative camber
* Increase front toe-out
* Increase rear toe-out
* Soften front suspension OR stiffen rear
* Soften front anti-roll bar OR stiffen rear bar
* Raise front ride height OR lower rear ride height
* Increase front wing aero
* Decrease front tire pressures
* Increase differential **off-throttle**
* Move brake bias backwards

#### **Oversteer (Car turns too much or spins)**

* Increase rear negative camber
* Increase front toe-in
* Decrease rear toe-out
* Stiffen front suspension OR soften rear
* Stiffen front anti-roll bar OR soften rear bar
* Raise rear ride height OR lower front
* Increase rear wing aero
* Decrease rear tire pressures
* Decrease differential **off-throttle**
* Move brake bias forwards

#### **Instability (Car is twitchy or unpredictable)**

* Reduce front and rear camber (closer to 0)
* Increase front toe-in and rear toe-in
* Soften both front and rear suspension and ARBs
* Raise rear ride height
* Increase rear wing aero
* Reduce rear tire pressure
* Reduce differential **off-throttle**
* Move brake bias forwards

---

### 🏁 Corner Exit (Acceleration Out of Turn)

#### **Understeer**

* Increase front toe-out
* Increase rear toe-out
* Soften front suspension OR stiffen rear
* Soften front ARB OR stiffen rear ARB
* Raise front ride height OR lower rear
* Increase front wing aero
* Decrease front tire pressures
* Increase differential **on-throttle**

#### **Oversteer**

* Increase rear negative camber
* Increase rear toe-in
* Stiffen front suspension OR soften rear
* Stiffen front ARB OR soften rear
* Lower front ride height OR raise front
* Increase rear wing aero
* Decrease rear tire pressures
* Decrease differential **on-throttle**

#### **Instability**

* Reduce rear camber (closer to 0)
* More rear toe-in
* Soften rear suspension
* Soften rear ARB
* Raise rear ride height
* Increase rear wing aero
* Reduce front & rear tire pressures
* Reduce differential **on-throttle**
* Move brake bias away from rear tires
* Increase brake pressure

---

## 🛣️ STRAIGHTS

### 🐢 Low End Acceleration

* Reduce front and rear toe
* Reduce front and rear ride height
* Reduce front and rear wing aero
* Reduce front and rear tire pressures
* Increase differential **on-throttle**

### 🚀 Top Speed (Top End)

* Reduce camber (closer to 0)
* Reduce toe
* Reduce ride height
* Reduce wing aero
* Reduce tire pressures
* Increase differential **on-throttle**

---

## 🛞 TYRES

### 🔥 Overheating

* Reduce camber
* Reduce toe
* Decrease tire pressure (if too high)
* Increase pressure (if too low)
* Reduce differential (on- and off-throttle)
* Move brake bias away from overheating tires
* Reduce brake pressure

### 🧊 Overcooling

* Increase camber
* Increase toe-out/in
* Increase tire pressure (if low)
* Decrease pressure (if high)
* Increase differential (on- and off-throttle)
* Increase brake pressure

---

## 🛑 BRAKES

### 🔓 Locking Under Braking

* Reduce brake pressure
* Move brake bias rearward
* Reduce front wing aero
* Reduce front tire pressure

### 😬 Instability While Braking

* Increase front toe-in
* Decrease front camber
* Increase front wing aero
* Reduce rear ride height
* Reduce brake pressure
* Move brake bias forward

---

## 🚧 OTHER HANDLING ISSUES

### ⬇️ Excessive Scraping / Bottoming Out

* Reduce ride height
* Reduce brake pressure
* Move brake bias forward

### ⛽ High Fuel Consumption

* Reduce toe
* Reduce wing aero
* Increase tire pressure

---

## 🌤️ RACE CONDITIONS

### 🔥 Excessive Tyre Degradation

* Reduce camber
* Reduce toe
* Increase ride height (lift tires off the road more)
* Reduce wing aero
* Increase tire pressures (if low)
* Move brake bias away from overheating tires

---

Each section offers targeted changes to optimize performance for specific symptoms you may experience during a race. Mix and match settings based on the track and your driving style!
