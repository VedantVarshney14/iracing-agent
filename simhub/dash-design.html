The trace: last lap and the fix, for the coming corner

The trace shows the next corner, from the approach to the exit, as soon as the previous corner is done, so you read it on the straight before you get there.
  • Bright lines are your brake (red) and throttle (green) from the last lap through this corner: the real recorded trace, not drawn live.
  • Wide soft bands with a dotted centre line behind them are the target: your lap with the coach's one adjustment applied. Here the brake comes 18 m later, so the lift and the brake shift right and the rest stays the same. Lay your line on the band.
  • The arrow above the plot runs from where you braked to where the target brakes. The dashed line marks the apex.
  • The cue above the trace says the same thing in a few words, in amber. No sentences on the wheel.
  • Curves are smoothed with no sharp corners, and a pedal at rest isn't drawn, so there's no line along the bottom. Every element has its own band of the screen: top row 18–122, cue 128–156, plot 160–244, lap strip 250–262.
The lap strip along the bottom: green gaining, amber losing a little, red losing a lot, grey not driven yet; the car moves left to right, a white notch marks the next corner, a purple bracket the focus corner.

What it needs

Per corner: last lap's brake and throttle sampled by distance (the recorder has them), the target trace (last lap with the adjustment applied, from the reference lap), and a cue of a few words. The feed would send the two traces as small arrays once per corner; the plugin exposes them as properties the dashboard plots.
